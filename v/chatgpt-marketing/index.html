How did ChatGPT get so popular so fast?

ChatGPT launched in November 2022 with no TV ads. Five days later, a million people were using it. Most of the technology wasn't new. The marketing was.

We see five moves behind it.

One: promise small. It was called a research preview, so mistakes were forgiven and good answers felt like surprises.

Two: change the box. OpenAI had sold similar models to developers since 2020, behind a wall of code. ChatGPT put one in a plain chat box.

Three: let the product be the ad. Every answer was something you could screenshot and share.

Four: sell the change, not the tool. It was sold as the start of a new age.

Five: build a price ladder. Free at the bottom, then Plus at $20 a month and Pro at $200.

A head start did not win the market for good, though. Google's Gemini has closed part of the gap.
