Why did Nvidia's boss come back, and who gained?

On 8 November 2026 Jensen Huang, Nvidia's boss, admitted an affair. CNBC reports the woman is an alleged Chinese spy. Nvidia's shares fell nearly a third and Huang resigned. On 17 November the board chair said the board did not intend to bring him back.

Then, on Sunday 22 November, President Trump gave the board a personal promise that Huang had not broken national security laws, "at least with everything we know." People close to the board told CNBC that this sealed it. On Monday the board took Huang back, every member voting yes, and the shares closed up 31%.

Trump's accounts owned up to 370,000 Nvidia shares. The Financial Times estimated his holding rose by up to $15 million that day. Huang also helped fund Trump's $300 million White House ballroom. No wrongdoing has been found, and Nvidia's own investigation goes on.
