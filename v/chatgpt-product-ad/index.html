How did ChatGPT get so popular without ads?

ChatGPT launched as a free research preview in November 2022. Its first TV ad, at the Super Bowl, came in February 2025. That is more than two years later, and by then it didn't need one.

The users did the spreading. Every answer was a screenshot, and every screenshot showed the product working.

In March 2025, a new image tool flooded the internet with pictures. Sam Altman, OpenAI's boss, posted: "our GPUs are melting." Most companies would love that problem. Four days later, he posted that ChatGPT had added one million users in the last hour.

The lesson is simple. Make your result easy to show off, and your users become your ads.
