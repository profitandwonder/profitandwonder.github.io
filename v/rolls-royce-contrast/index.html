Why does Rolls-Royce show its cars next to private jets?

A Rolls-Royce costs over $400,000. That sounds huge, until you see what's next to it.

Rory Sutherland of the ad agency Ogilvy says that after a day of looking at eighty-million-dollar jets, the car is basically an impulse buy. Rolls-Royce has shown a car at the Monaco Yacht Show, back in 2016.

That's the contrast effect. A price feels big or small next to what you saw just before it.

A 2025 Columbia study of over 2,000 US adults shows it with much smaller money. 74% said they'd drive ten minutes to save $10 on a $30 calculator. On a $250 jacket, only 34% would. Same ten dollars. It just looks smaller next to a bigger price.

So when a price looks small, check what's next to it.

It's like putting the sweets next to the counter." Found only second-hand: George Mack on X, 2021. Sources: x.com, supercarblondie.com

  4. Jet price Gulfstream G700 list price about $79–80M. Source: flycraft.com
  5. Save $5 with a 20-minute drive; 68% would go for a $15 calculator (N = 93), 29% for a $125 one (N = 88). Tversky & Kahneman, "The Framing of Decisions and the Psychology of Choice", Science 211, 453–458 (1981), Problem 10 Source: gwern.net
