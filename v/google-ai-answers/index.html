Videos · Google and search AI and marketing

Do people still click on links when Google shows an AI answer?

Far fewer of them do. Pew Research Center tracked real Google searches made by 900 U.S. adults in March 2025.

With no AI summary on the page, people clicked a regular search result in 15% of visits. With an AI summary on top, that fell to 8%. Nearly half the clicks, gone.

The links inside the AI summary did even worse. They were clicked in just 1% of visits.

So many people read the answer and stop there. The answer is the new shop window.

How do you get into it? Google says there are no extra requirements or special tricks to appear in its AI features. Its advice is the usual one: write helpful, people-first pages that answer real questions clearly.

One honest note: Pew measured clicks, not which pages get quoted. "Be the page the AI quotes" is our takeaway from Google's advice, not a Pew finding.
