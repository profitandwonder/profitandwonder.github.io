How do Google's AI answers hurt its own ad business?

Search ads bring in about half of Alphabet's money, and those ads live on your click. For years, Google fought anyone who tried to game the free results: stuffed keywords, fake pages, bought links, cheap articles.

Then Google built a better answer. Since May 2024, AI Overviews answer many questions right on the page. Pew Research found that people clicked a normal link 8% of the time when an AI summary appeared, and 15% of the time without one.

In October 2026, Alphabet's shares fell about 13% in a day. Investors were scared by the AI spending, and search ads grew but missed what Wall Street expected. A top boss blamed that partly on AI Mode, which shows fewer ads.

Google's founders saw it coming. In 1998 they wrote: "the better the search engine is, the fewer advertisements will be needed." They sold the ads anyway.
