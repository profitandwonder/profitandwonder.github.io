What would a trillion dollars of paperclips look like?

A pack of 1,000 standard paperclips cost $15.29 at Staples on 17 November 2026. At that price, a trillion dollars buys about 65 trillion paperclips.

Clip them end to end and the chain is about 2.1 billion km long: from the Sun to past Saturn. But a chain is thin wire. One paperclip weighs under half a gram.

Melt them down and you get about 28 million tonnes of steel: one cube about 150 metres on each side. The world makes that much steel in under 6 days.

Steel wire sells for about $500 a tonne, so the block is worth about $14 billion. That is under 2 cents of every dollar you paid.

Our view: the other 98 cents paid for the work. Someone bent the wire, boxed it, trucked it and put it on a shelf. Nobody has 65 trillion paperclips in stock, so this is a game.
