What did GTA 6's launch day cost work?

GTA V came out in September 2013. Its follow-up, GTA VI, came out on Thursday 19 November 2026, after two delays (autumn 2025, then May 2026). Fans waited 13 years.

The hype grew while they waited. The first trailer leaked, so Rockstar put it out a day early, and it set a Guinness world record: 90 million views in 24 hours.

So when the day came, a lot of fans did not wait one more day. BambooHR saw paid time off requests for 19 November about 13% higher than the same days in 2024 and 2025. In its survey, almost a third of Gen Z workers planned the day off, and half of the people taking time off for a game planned to call in sick.

In the UK alone, a Uswitch/OnePoll survey put the missed work at about 20.5 million hours. Our sum: that is about 2,340 years of nonstop time. Most of it is booked holiday, so it is a cost of time, not a fine.

The longer people wait for something, the more of their life they give it when it comes.
