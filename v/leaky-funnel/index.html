What is a marketing funnel and why does it leak?

Every business has a funnel. And every funnel leaks.

People pour in at the top. First they notice you. Then they get interested. Then they want it. Then they buy. Marketers call these steps AIDA: attention, interest, desire, action.

But at every step, people slip out. Of all the people who see your ad, only a small share ever buys. The exact rate varies a lot by business.

The leaks are real. Online, about seven in ten shoppers fill a cart, then leave without paying. That's the average of 50 studies collected by the Baymard Institute in 2025.

So don't just pour in more people. Find the biggest leak, and fix it.
