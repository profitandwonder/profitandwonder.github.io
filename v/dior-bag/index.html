How much does it cost to make a €2,600 Dior bag?

The work can cost very little. In June 2024, a Milan court found that a contractor could make one Dior handbag for Dior for as little as €53. Dior sold that bag in its shops for €2,600.

We know because police checked Dior's contractors for worker abuse. The court found that workers slept at the workshop, so the machines could run 24 hours a day.

The rest pays for leather, shops and, mostly, the name. In 2025, fashion and leather goods at LVMH, Dior's owner (Louis Vuitton included), made €13.2 billion of profit.

The court put Dior's Italian bag maker under a court-named administrator for a year, for not checking its suppliers. Dior was not charged with a crime.

The fine print: the €53 is the workshop's price for the work, not Dior's full cost. "Mostly the name" is our reading of the gap.
