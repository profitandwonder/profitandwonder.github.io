Why did New Coke flop if people liked the taste better?

Before it changed its recipe, Coca-Cola ran taste tests on nearly 200,000 people. They preferred the new formula. So in April 1985, Coke swapped its original recipe, first made in 1886, for the new one.

People were furious. By June, calls to Coke's consumer hotline had gone from 400 a day to 1,500 a day. Some people hoarded as many as 900 bottles of the old Coke in their basements.

On July 11, 1985, just 79 days later, the original was back as Coca-Cola Classic.

Coke's own history page says what went wrong: the tests didn't show the bond people felt with their Coca-Cola. They measured taste, not meaning.

Don't just test your product. Test what it means to people.
