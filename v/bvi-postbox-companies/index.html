Can anyone set up an offshore company, and does it save tax?

Anyone can set up a company in the British Virgin Islands through a local agent, for about $1,500 in the first year. The islands have about 40,000 people and about 354,000 companies: roughly nine for every person. Fees from these companies pay for about 61% of the government.

For someone who works in the UK, it saves nothing. Tax comes off your pay before you get it. And a company run from the UK counts as a UK company for tax.

It helps people who really live elsewhere and own shares, businesses or property through the company. The islands take no company tax, and the real owner's name is not on a public list.

All of this is legal. The UK closed one gap: since 2022, a foreign company that owns UK property must name its real owner in public. Everything else is still hidden.
