Why does Goldman Sachs want Anthropic's staff more than its IPO fee?

An IPO is when a company sells its shares to the public for the first time. Anthropic, the company behind Claude, is about to do that, and Goldman Sachs is one of the two banks that lead the sale.

One analyst estimates Goldman's fee at about $79 million. The sale could raise up to $64 billion, so that is roughly one dollar of every thousand, and it is paid once.

The sale also makes new rich people. Thousands of Anthropic staff are paid partly in shares. Rich people pay a bank to look after their money, and the bank takes a small slice every year. Goldman's asset and wealth arm earned $3.6 billion in such fees in one quarter.

Four other big banks talked to Anthropic about its staff too. Anthropic asked the banks to send their fees.

The fine print: the sale had not priced when we made this video. The $79 million is an estimate, not a filed number. Nobody has said that any Anthropic employee is a Goldman client.
